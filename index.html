South Carolina closings are handled by licensed closing attorneys. Buyers often budget roughly 2% to 4% of the purchase price for lender fees, attorney fees, title insurance and prepaid taxes and insurance. Sellers typically pay the deed recording fee ($3.70 per $1,000 of the sales price), deed preparation and agreed brokerage compensation. James can walk you through an estimate for your specific home before you list or make an offer.
Yes. Selling and buying at once comes down to timing and financing. Options include a sale contingency on your purchase, a short post-closing occupancy for the seller, or lining up financing that does not depend on the first sale. James helps you weigh those options with your lender before you commit.
Before real estate, James worked in mortgage finance. That background shapes how he reads an offer's financing, plans around appraisal and underwriting timelines, and explains the numbers clearly so there are fewer surprises before closing.
A home that is your legal residence in South Carolina can be assessed at 4% of its value and is exempt from school operating millage. Second homes, rentals and investment property are assessed at 6% without that exemption, so the tax bill is noticeably higher. Buyers must apply with the county assessor to get the 4% rate.
